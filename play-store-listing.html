About this game

Take on an exhilarating endless 3D run through 10 dynamic color-themed worlds! Swipe to switch lanes, jump over hazards, slide beneath barriers, and collect glowing coins as the track accelerates.

Key Features:

  • 10 Ever-Changing Environments: Transitions automatically every 300m from Ancient Temples to Cyber Neon Cities and Space Platforms.
  • 4 Super Power-Ups: Magnet, Invincibility Shield, 2X Coin Boost, and 2X Score Multiplier.
  • 6 Cyber Outfits: Unlock and equip custom skins in the Character Shop.
  • Smooth Controls: Intuitive swipe gestures for mobile touchscreen and keyboard WASD/Arrow support.
  • Offline Playable: Enjoy seamless action anywhere with local high score tracking.
