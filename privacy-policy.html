Privacy Policy

Official Production Release Policy — MRU Infinite Runner 3D

Effective Date: January 1, 2025
App Name: MRU Infinite Runner 3D
Developer / Contact: MRU Games Studio (support@mruinfiniterunner.com)

1. Overview

MRU Games Studio ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to respecting the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled in connection with the mobile application MRU Infinite Runner 3D ("the App"). The App operates primarily as an offline 3D endless runner game with local progress saving and optional Google Mobile Ads integration.

2. Data Collection and Local Storage

The core gameplay of MRU Infinite Runner 3D does not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect or store personal identifiers such as your real name, email address, physical address, or phone number on any external server controlled by us.

  • Local Save Data: Your high score, accumulated coins, unlocked character outfits, selected world preferences, volume settings, and daily reward claims are saved locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs.
  • Data Retention & Removal: All locally stored game data remains strictly on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the App will delete your local progress.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDK Disclosures

The App incorporates optional advertising services provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

  • Google AdMob: When enabled, Google Mobile Ads may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP addresses, location data, performance diagnostics, and ad interaction data to serve contextual or personalized advertisements and prevent fraud.
  • Consent Management (UMP): In regions governed by GDPR, CCPA, or applicable privacy legislation, the Google UMP consent form will be presented to allow you to grant or manage your ad privacy preferences.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

MRU Infinite Runner 3D is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Advertising content delivered through Google Mobile Ads is filtered in compliance with applicable children's protection standards.

5. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your information. Because all gameplay data is retained locally on your mobile device, risks associated with remote network storage are minimized. Communication between Google SDKs and ad servers occurs over encrypted HTTPS channels.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data choices:

  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or opt-out of personalized ads via your device's Android Settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Consent Modification: Re-open in-app settings at any time to re-configure consent choices.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions, feedback, or privacy concerns regarding MRU Infinite Runner 3D, please contact us at:

Email: support@mruinfiniterunner.com
Website: www.mruinfiniterunner.com
